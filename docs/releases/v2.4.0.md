# eURC POS v2.4.0 — payments and the GST/HST report

## What's new

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| **Payment methods** | The register offers the methods the Admin accepts (*Business → Payment methods*): **cash, debit, credit, Interac e-Transfer, Stripe**. The POS records how the customer paid; it never charges a card or moves money, and no card number is ever entered (no PCI scope). Debit and credit: the terminal approval code (credit: optional card brand). e-Transfer: the dialog shows the amount and the store's e-Transfer e-mail, the cashier enters the reference number from the deposit notification. Stripe: the payment id (`pi_…`, `ch_…`, `py_…`). The API refuses a method the store does not accept. |
| **Receipts and refunds** | Receipts name the method (e.g. *Credit card · Visa*) and its reference. A refund goes back the way the sale was paid, and the refund dialog says how: cash from the drawer, reverse on the terminal, send an e-Transfer back, or refund in the Stripe dashboard. |
| **Daily report** | One row per payment method used that day. |
| **GST/HST report** (new tab, Admin and Manager) | Any month, quarter, year or custom period, in the store's time zone: sales and tax **by tax rate** (0 %, GST 5 %, HST 13 %, …), refunds given in the period, payments by method, and the two GST/HST return lines - **line 101** (sales and other revenue, before tax, after refunds, zero-rated included) and **line 103** (GST/HST collected, net of tax refunded). Shows the legal name and GST/HST number from the business profile. *Export to Excel*: Summary, By tax rate, Payments. Summed in SQL (BIGINT), so a year of a 200,000-product supermarket never travels to the app. Input tax credits (line 106) come from purchase invoices and are not part of it. |
| **Store details stay current** | The till refreshes the store's payment methods and business details when it opens, so an Admin's change reaches tills that were already signed in. |

## Database

Migration 7 (expand only):

* `sales.payment_type` (exact method) and `sales.payment_brand`, with a CHECK on the
  type added `WITH NOCHECK` (no scan of existing rows during the migration).
* **Dual write for rollback safety:** `sales.payment_method` and `refunds.method`
  keep the values v2.0-v2.3 understand (`cash` / `card`); debit, credit,
  e-Transfer and Stripe are written there as `card`. Rows written by older
  versions have no `payment_type` and read back as before.
* Indexes for the report: `ix_refunds_store_created` and a filtered
  `ix_sales_store_refunded` (v2.0 whole-sale refunds by refund date).

Rollback caveat: on v2.3, sales paid by debit, credit, e-Transfer or Stripe show
as "Card", and its refund dialog says "reverse on the card terminal" for
e-Transfer and Stripe sales too. Nothing breaks; roll forward to restore the
exact names.
