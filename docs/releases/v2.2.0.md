# eURC POS v2.2.0 — store management

Includes v2.1 (item refunds). One pull request, one QA round, one production release.

## What's new

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| **Refunds (v2.1)** | Refund chosen items and quantities from a receipt, in several steps (`R-1001-1`, `R-1001-2`). Refunds always add up exactly to the sale. Receipts show refunds, net paid and a PARTLY REFUNDED stamp. |
| **Refund policy** | Each product is *Refundable* or *Final sale* (category **Food** defaults to final sale). Store refund window, default **30 days** (Staff page). Outside the policy, a manager may **override with a written reason**; overrides are recorded on the refund and counted in the report. The policy line is printed on every receipt. |
| **Excel import / export** | Products → *Import from Excel* (.xlsx or .csv): preview, per-row errors, nothing saved unless every row is valid; existing SKUs updated, new SKUs added. *Export to Excel* and a template. |
| **Batch price / tax** | Products → *Change prices / tax*: ticked products, a category, or all; set price, ± %, ± $, set tax %; always previewed before applying. |
| **Single product** | *Edit* price and tax inline; toggle Refundable / Final sale. |
| **Promotions** | Promotions tab: % off or $ off each, for all products, a category or chosen SKUs, with start and last day. Best promotion per line applies automatically; tax on the discounted price. Register shows promo prices, receipt shows *You saved*, report shows discounts. |
| **Security** | `@azure/msal-node` forced to ^7.1.1 (GHSA-xcx6-jx8p-x3h5, high). |

## Database

Migrations 4 and 5, **expand only** (new tables, NULL-able or defaulted columns). Applied automatically at start-up under an application lock.
Rollback caveat: after a swap back to v2.0/v2.1, pause refunds of promoted, final-sale or part-refunded receipts until v2.2 is back (roll forward).

## QA script for the test environment

1. **Products → Import from Excel**: download the template, add 3 rows (one in category Food), import. Food row shows *Final sale*.
2. **Change prices / tax**: tick 2 products, +10 %, Preview, Apply.
3. **Edit** one product's price inline.
4. **Promotions**: 20 % off one SKU, starting today.
5. **Register**: sell the promoted item + the Food item; check promo price, *You saved*, policy line on the receipt.
6. **Sales → receipt → Refund items**: the Food line is locked; refund the promoted item → amount = discounted price paid.
7. Tick *Override*, enter a reason, refund the Food item.
8. **Report**: promotion discounts, refunded, net sales, 1 policy override.
9. **Staff**: set the refund window to 14 days; new receipts say 14 days.
